---
title: "Thai Red Curry"
categories: ["entrees"]
---

## Ingredients

- 2 red or sweet potatoes
- 3 large carrots, diced
- 0.50 medium onion, diced
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 2.50 cups water
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup red lentils
- 14 oz can diced tomatoes
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 5 tbsp red curry paste
- 0.50 tsp fine salt

## Directions

1. Cook potatoes, carrots, and onion for about 3 minutes.

2. Stir in ginger and garlic.

3. Add water.

4. Stir in lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk, curry paste, and salt.

5. Cook until lentils and veggies are tender (20ish minutes).
