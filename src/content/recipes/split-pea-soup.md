---
title: "Split Pea Soup"
categories: ["soups-stews"]
---

**Yield:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs dried green or yellow split peas, rinsed
- 1 onion
- 3 celery ribs
- 2 large carrots
- 1 garlic clove
- ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley
- 6 cups water or vegetable broth (water works just fine)
- ½ tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp salt if using water, less if using broth, or to taste
- 1 bay leaf

## Directions

1. Combine all ingredients.

2. Cook in a slow cooker on low for 7 to 8 hours, OR

3. in a slow cooker on high for 3 to 4 hours, OR

4. in an instant pot for 15 min.
