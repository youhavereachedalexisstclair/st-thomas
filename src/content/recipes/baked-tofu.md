---
title: "Baked Tofu"
categories: ["entrees","sides"]
---

**Yield:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 block extra-firm tofu, drained and cut into ¼” slices
- 0.50 cup tamari
- 1 cup nutritional yeast
- oil for greasing pan or parchment paper

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 425 °F.

2. Dip tofu slices in tamari, then coat each slice in nutritional
yeast.

3. Bake on a greased sheet pan (or line pan with baking parchment) for 20-25
minutes.

4. Tofu baked in this way may be served warm or cold: on its own, on a
sandwich, or cut in strips on a salad.
